Common questions

How do we pair?

One of you shows a six-character code or a QR code from the app. The other types the code or scans it.

Can I undo unpairing?

Yes. Unpairing can be undone for 30 days.

Can my partner read my journal?

No. Journal text, including past nights, is only readable by its writer. Your partner sees word counts and mood.

Where did my pet go?

Pets leave if they're neglected. Call them home once a day, three days in a row, and they come back.

How do I delete my data?

Delete your account in the app. Your data is erased after 30 days unless you sign back in. More in the privacy policy.

Can I turn off usage counts?

Yes, in the app's settings. They're simple counts sent to our own server, never to a third party.

Nudges aren't arriving.

Check that notifications are on for Partner in Crimes in iPhone Settings, then open the app once.
