Common questions

How do we pair?

One of you shares a pairing code from the app. The other enters it. That's it.

Can I undo unpairing?

Yes. Unpairing can be undone for 30 days.

Can my partner read my journal?

No. Journal text is only readable by its writer. Your partner sees stats only.

Where did my pet go?

Pets leave if they're neglected. Call them home once a day, three days in a row, and they come back.

How do I export or delete my data?

Both are in the app. Deleted data is erased after 30 days unless you sign back in. More in the privacy policy.

Nudges aren't arriving.

Check that notifications are on for Partner in Crimes in iPhone Settings, then open the app once.
