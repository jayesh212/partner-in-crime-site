Privacy policy

What we keep, and who sees it.

Plain words. No ads, no tracking, no fine print hiding behind fine print.

The short version

  • No ads. No tracking across apps. No third-party analytics.
  • Your journal is yours. Not even your partner can read it.
  • Delete everything from the app, with 30 days to change your mind.

What we keep

Account
Your name and an email address, so we can keep your account and your partner knows it's you.
What you add
Tasks, water, challenges, thank-you notes, moods, messages in The Hideout, drawings, game turns, your streak, and your home with its pets, plants and things.
Photos
Photos you post to Moments or The Hideout. We store a smaller, reduced-resolution copy, not your original.
Journal
Wind-down entries. Only you can read the text, including your past nights. Your partner sees your word count and mood, never your words.
Apple Health
Only if you allow it: your daily step count. Nothing else from Health.
Notifications
A push token from your iPhone, so nudges and reminders reach you. Turn them off any time in iPhone Settings.
Usage counts
Simple counts of which screens are opened and which buttons are tapped, sent to our own server to help us fix and improve the app. No third-party analytics service is involved, and you can switch this off in the app's settings.

What your partner sees

What you share on purpose: shared tasks, water, challenges, thank-you notes, photos, messages, games, your streak and your home. Stats and moods appear only as described above. Private tasks and journal text stay private.

Where it lives

On Amazon Web Services in the US (Oregon, us-west-2), with photos in Amazon S3, and in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Aiven. These providers store data for us. They don't use it for anything else.

What we never do

Sell your data. Show ads. Track you across other apps or websites. Use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Share Health data with anyone.

Unpair and delete

Unpair
Can be undone for 30 days.
Delete
Delete your account in the app. Your data is erased after 30 days, unless you sign back in before then.

Children

Partner in Crimes is not made for children under 13.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date below. Questions: Support.
