Questions

Is it only for couples?

No. Couples first, but it works for any two people: friends, siblings, training partners.

Do we both need an iPhone?

Yes. Each of you uses your own iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

What can my partner see?

Shared tasks, water, steps, streaks, photos you share and journal stats. Not your private tasks or your journal text.

When can I get it?

It's in final testing. The App Store link will appear on this page the day it's live.
