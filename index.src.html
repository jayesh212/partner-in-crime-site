    of inactive men got active when their partner did too.

Questions

Fine print.

What is Partner in Crimes?

An iPhone app for couples to do things together: shared and private to-dos, a water tracker for two, four-week couple challenges, a thank-you wall, a shared streak, games, a little home with pets and plants, and a 15-minute journal you start at the same time.

Is it only for couples?

No. Made for couples, and it works just as well for best friends, roommates and accountability buddies.

Do we both need an iPhone?

Yes. Each of you uses your own iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

What can my partner see?

Shared tasks, water, steps, streaks, the photos and notes you share, and your journal stats. Not your private tasks or what you write at night.

Can I try it alone first?

Yes. You can pair later. Some games can be played against the house until then.

When can I get it?
